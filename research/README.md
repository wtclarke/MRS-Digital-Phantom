# Notebook-related research

The supported simulation examples are in [headless_simulation.ipynb](../notebooks/headless_simulation.ipynb), with [legacy BASIS-water instructions](../docs/legacy_basis_water_reference.md).

[Optional validation](validation/README.md) retains BASIS provenance checks, the saved legacy-water LCModel experiment and the TE97 concentration-order diagnosis. Production simulation imports no research modules and requires none of their generated outputs.

Saved notebook-related outputs remain local under ignored `outputs/`. External BASIS/MAT files and licensed LCModel executables are supplied separately; they are not bundled or downloaded.
